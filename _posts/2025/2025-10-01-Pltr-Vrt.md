Here are a couple buyable stocks :)

Everyone's favorite AI mass surveillance company Palantir Technologies Inc. (PLTR) has formed a nice cup and handle, a bullish pattern. Some highlights of the chart include the high volume jump in August. Afterwards, the stock fell but found support at its 50 day moving average (DMA), again on high volume. The company's had several quarters of 100%+ earnings growth and the stock looks to push into new highs soon. Yay

I rationalize/moralize this stock selection via a simple "me making money is good." 🤷🏼‍♂️🤦‍♂️

Data center infrastructure company Vertiv Holdings LLC (VRT) broke out of a similar cup and handle pattern today on increasing volume. The included infographic shows Vertiv is a big player in critical sectors of AI data center construction, including power distribution and HVAC. Massive AI data centers will be built over the next 5-10 years. For example, Mark Zuckerburg's Meta is planning two 5GW data centers, each roughly half the size of Manhattan 😳. Vertiv looks to capitalize, it's most recent quarter saw an 80% increase in earnings per share (EPS) when compared to last year.
