Bitcoin! The leading cryptocurrency is setting up nicely. A cup and handle has formed this year, following two high-volume upward moves in 2023 and 2024. Bitcoin's recent price action is marked by two clusters of weekly tight closes with low volume. It has also been respecting its weekly averages and remains within long term trends. All very constructive!!

Bitcoin can be purchased through various brokers, such as Robinhood, or via ETFs such as IBIT.

Ethereum is also having a strong run, rushing back to all time highs. To me, it's chart is not as strong, but it may provide higher returns for a higher risk. I don't bother with other Altcoins.
