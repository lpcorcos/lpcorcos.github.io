American Eagle Outfitters, Inc. (ticker AEO) rose 38% today after an earnings beat. Its exciting new ad campaign is driving sales, and squeezing some shorts ;). Forget AI, is retail the place to be? 

AEO is heavily shorted, at 18% of the float. From sorority rush videos to endorsements by President Donald Trump, the shorts may have underestimated the cultural shift caused by the new "great jeans" ad campaign. In fashion, what's hot sells best. The bears will have to get out of the way or incur heavy losses. In doing so, they will take the stock price up with them (Also note, short sellers will often double down and re-short, refusing to admit they are wrong).

The chart also demonstrates strength; a cup and handle can be seen from May through August, albeit after a downtrend. The stock peaked through its 200-day moving average before its strong earnings. Today's price movement was from huge...volume, and the downtrend is reversed. It may take some time for the stock to digest these gains, but short squeezes are explosive, see e.g., Gamestop. The stock currently trades at $18.79 per share. Tentatively, a stock price of $50 is very achievable by 2027.
