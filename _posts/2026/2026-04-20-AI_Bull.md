What a rally! The war is (effectively) over and the true AI bull market is here!

<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
After the cease-fire between the United States and Iran was announced, the market surged.
Despite threats of armageddon, the two countries are negotiating passage through the Straight of Hormuz. Oil infrastructure has been left undisturbed, indicating to the market that, from the US and President Trump's perspective, the war is effectively over. There will be bumps in the road, but a deal will likely be reached, both sides will declare victory, and what's left is the new AI bull market.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
The market rally is stunning, and is one I have been waiting for. The Nasdaq led the charge, rallying 12% in two weeks on high increasing volume. This indicates that hi-tech AI stocks are the strongest choice. Earnings per share (EPS) growth for AI-related companies is off the charts (see below). Additionally, several fresh IPO's such as SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI are in the works. Combine this revolutionary technology with a pro-business president, with military contracts, and with several men fighting for trillionaire status, this could be a bull market for the ages.
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify; text-justify: inter-word;">
