The war with Iran has sparked a wave of selling along with high oil prices. We can see selling with high red volume on the weekly and daily charts of the S&P 500. There was a brief reprieve on this holiday shortened week, with the market holding its 1year moving average (the blue line). However we note the volume was low.

The daily chart shows we are on the third day of the rally attempt, will it hold? Over the next few weeks expect a high volume move that will determine the market direction for the next several months.

<div align="center">
		<img src="/images/stocks/2026/SPX_04-06-26_Daily.png" alt="Spx" style="width:50%">
		<br>
		<small>The daily chart of the S&P 500. We are on the third day of a rally attempt.</small>
</div>
<br>
If the war ends soon, expect a rally going into fresh IPO's such as SpaceX, Anthropic, and OpenAI. This could lead to a new bull market. Sandisk (SNDK) also sits highly on my watch list.

However should the war continue, and if energy infrastructure is heavily damaged, expect a very sharp fall. 30% of the world's oil is at risk. The shocks could be unprecedented.
