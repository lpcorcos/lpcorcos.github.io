Broadly, my research interests include mathematical modeling and scientific computing. During my Ph.D., I specialized in numerical partial differential equations (PDEs), and developed high-order accurate algorithms for multi-physics interface problems.

Discontinuous Galerkin Methods

As part of my work on multi-physics multi-layer coating flows (see below), I developed local discontinuous Galerkin methods (LDG) for Poisson problems with Robin boundary conditions. For this, consider a \(d\)-dimensional domain \( \Omega \subset \mathbb{R}^d \), with Robin boundary conditions applied along the boundary \( \Gamma_R \), we wish to find \(u:\Omega \rightarrow \mathbb{R} \) such that

\[ \begin{cases} -\nabla \cdot ( \mu \nabla u) = f &\text{ in } \Omega \\ au + (b \mu \nabla u)\cdot \textbf{n} = r &\text{ on } \Gamma_R, \end{cases} \] where \(\mu\) is the diffusion coefficient, \(a,b\) are Robin coefficients, \(f,r\) are source terms, and \(\textbf{n} \) is the normal vector to \(\Gamma_R \).

Our resulting LDG discretization has several favorable properties: it is optimally high-order accurate in the maximum norm and the final linear system is symmetric positive definite. Moreover, the LDG discretization is amenable to straightforward and fast multigrid solvers. These LDG methods are used with the Implicit Mesh DG framework to apply Robin boundary conditions along curved interfaces defined implicitly via the level set method, which are in turn used to capture the effects of evaporation within multi-layer coating flow problems, to high-order accuracy. Additionally, I developed high-order accurate LDG methods for Poisson problems with Robin jump conditions as well as for Stokes problems with Navier-slip boundary conditions. These can be found in my thesis.
