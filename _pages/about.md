I am an active stock trader and have independently researched the market since 2016. 
My AAR since 2020 is 32%. 
You can follow my stock and crypto ideas on my [Blog](/stocks/).


I received my Ph.D. in [Applied Mathematics](https://math.berkeley.edu/home) from the University of California Berkeley. During that time, 
I was a Graduate Student Researcher (GSR) in the [Mathematics Group](https://crd.lbl.gov/divisions/amcr/mathematics-dept/math/) at the Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory. I have a B.Sc in [Mathematics](https://www-math.umd.edu/) and [Physics](https://umdphysics.umd.edu/) from the University of Maryland College Park, with High Honors in physics.

My research interests include numerical partial differential equations (PDEs) and scientific computing, with a focus on multi-physics interface problems. My thesis work involved developing high-order accurate numerical methods for interfacial fluid dynamics coupled to heat and mass transfer processes.
